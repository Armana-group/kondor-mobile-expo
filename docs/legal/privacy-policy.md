# Privacy Policy

**Kondor Wallet**
Last updated: November 27, 2024

## Overview

Kondor Wallet ("the App") is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet for the Koinos blockchain, developed by Armana ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## Our Commitment to Privacy

Kondor Wallet is designed with privacy as a core principle. As a non-custodial wallet:

- **We do not have access to your private keys or recovery phrase**
- **We do not create accounts or require registration**
- **We do not operate servers that store your personal data**

## Information Storage

### Data Stored on Your Device

All sensitive information is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers:

- **Recovery phrase (seed phrase)** - Encrypted and stored in your device's secure storage
- **Private keys** - Encrypted and stored in your device's secure storage
- **Encrypted wallet backups** - Backup files may include encrypted recovery phrases and private keys. Kondor never stores or receives the plaintext recovery phrase, private keys, or backup recovery key.
- **Wallet addresses** - Stored locally for display purposes
- **Contacts** - Stored locally on your device
- **Transaction history** - Cached locally for performance

This data remains on your device and is protected by your device's security features, including biometric authentication when enabled.

### Data We Do Not Collect

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Private keys or recovery phrases
- Transaction details

## Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following third-party services to function:

### Koinos Blockchain Network
- **Purpose:** To query balances, broadcast transactions, and retrieve blockchain data
- **Data sent:** Your public wallet address and signed transactions
- **Provider:** Public Koinos RPC nodes (api.koinos.io, api.koinosblocks.com)

### KoinDX Token Registry
- **Purpose:** To fetch the list of available tokens and their metadata
- **Data sent:** No personal data; only requests token list
- **Provider:** KoinDX (GitHub API)

### Koiner.app
- **Purpose:** To display transaction history
- **Data sent:** Your public wallet address (to query transactions)
- **Provider:** Koiner.app API

These services may have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

## Blockchain Transparency

Please note that blockchain transactions are public by nature. When you send or receive tokens:

- Your public wallet address is visible on the blockchain
- Transaction amounts and timestamps are publicly recorded
- This is inherent to blockchain technology and not within our control

## Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

- Private keys and recovery phrases are stored in encrypted secure storage
- Biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint) is supported
- The App does not transmit sensitive data over the network

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to delete, modify, or export. Your wallet data is entirely under your control on your device.

To remove all App data, simply uninstall the application from your device.
